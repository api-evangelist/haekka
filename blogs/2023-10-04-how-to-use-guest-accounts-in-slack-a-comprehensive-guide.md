Slack is a powerful communication tool that allows teams to collaborate effectively on projects and tasks. One of the features that makes Slack so useful is the ability to invite guests to join your workspace. Guest accounts are a great way to bring external partners, vendors, and clients into your channels and conversations without giving them full access to your team’s conversations and files.
