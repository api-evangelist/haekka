Phishing attacks are a major concern for organizations today, as perpetrators use deceptive emails and fraudulent websites to compromise sensitive data or gain unauthorized access to systems. In response, many organizations have turned to phishing simulations to train employees and gauge their susceptibility to such attacks. But does conducting phishing simulations help companies comply with the SOC 2 common criteria?
