Hook Security has partnered with Haekka to make its security awareness training available directly within Slack. This collaboration allows users to easily access essential cybersecurity and HIPAA training without leaving their daily workspace, making it simpler for companies and managed service providers (MSPs) to improve security practices. With early excitement from customers, especially in healthcare and finance, this partnership aims to build stronger security habits by integrating training into employees’ everyday routines.
