In today's distributed modern work world, companies face a myriad of dynamic cybersecurity threats. These threats often target the weakest link in an organization: the human factor. Managed security awareness is a proactive approach to cybersecurity education, providing continuous training and reinforcement of best practices for employees, reducing the risk of human error, and ultimately improving an organization's overall security posture.
