# Haekka

> Slack-native microlearning that delivers short, timely training inside Slack to improve knowledge, compliance, and team performance.

Additional context: Haekka focuses on training in the flow of work. Core features include Training, Streams, Workflows, Engagements, and Phishing. Audience includes L&D, Security, Compliance, and team leaders at SaaS companies.

## Glossary
- **Training** — Course content and assessments delivered inside Slack.
- **Streams** — Continuous micro-content to reinforce behaviors.
- **Workflows** — Event-based rules that assign training or send nudges.
- **Engagements** — Lightweight prompts and check-ins that gather feedback.
- **Phishing** — Slack-native phishing education and simulations.
